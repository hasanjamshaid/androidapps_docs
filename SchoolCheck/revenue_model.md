# SchoolCheck (اسکول چیک) expected Revenue Model

This document outlines the expected revenue model, monetization channels, target customer segments, and financial scenarios for **SchoolCheck (اسکول چیک)**, the independent parent network and daycare/school auditing platform in Pakistan.

---

## 1. Target Customer Segments & Local Context

Private education in Pakistan is a multi-billion rupee industry. Middle and upper-income parents pay high monthly tuition fees to top school chains (Beaconhouse, LGS, roots, City School) and premium daycares. However, school chains routinely conceal hidden operational costs, high teacher turnover, and incident tracking data. SchoolCheck aggregates crowdsourced reviews, Google Maps data, and verified parent posts to output a transparent "Reality Check Scorecard".

- **Primary Audience**:
  - Parents of toddlers/infants selecting daycares or preschools.
  - Middle-to-upper-class families searching for private primary and secondary schools.
  - Parents of children requiring private home tuition or test preparation.
- **Core Value Proposition**: Fee transparency (hidden costs), teacher turnover stability tracking, bullying/incident handling ratings, transit congestion estimates, and verified van registries.

---

## 2. Primary Monetization Streams

SchoolCheck operates on an ad-supported freemium model. Free users generate display ad impressions on scorecards and forums, with premium upgrades for detailed cost calculations, van driver contacts, B2B tutor matching, and admission callback referrals.

### A. Targeted Display Ads (AdMob)
- **Details**: Contextual display ads shown below scorecards and forum posts.
- **Pakistan CPM**: **$0.40 USD** (approx. 111 PKR).
- **Engagement**: Users browse multiple reviews and scorecards, averaging 6 check-in sessions/month, viewing 10 pages/session (60 ad impressions per free user/month).

### B. SchoolCheck Pro (Premium B2C Subscriptions)
- **Features unlocked**:
  - Full access to the **True Annual Cost Calculator** (estimating annual resources, uniforms, mandatory vendors, and O/A Level British Council exam registration fees).
  - Detailed transit delay zones and route registries.
  - Verified local school van directory and driver ratings/contacts.
  - Access to the secure, anonymous administrative complaint board.
- **Pricing**: **200 PKR / month** (approx. $0.72 USD) or **1,500 PKR / year**.
- **Target Conversion**: **1.0% of MAU**.

### C. Daycare & School Admission Referral Leads (B2B Transactions)
- **Details**: Premium daycares and Montessori centers paying a bounty for verified parent callbacks or tour booking inquiries generated directly through the app.
- **Referral Payout**: **1,000 PKR** per qualified tour booking referral.
- **Target Conversion**: **0.25% of MAU** book a tour monthly.

### D. Home Tutors & Academy Placements (B2B Sponsors)
- **Details**: Home tutors, academy networks (e.g. Kip's, local O/A level tuition centers), and specialized test preparation classes pay a monthly listing fee to pin their contact cards to relevant school profiles (e.g., showing a top Physics tutor next to Lahore Grammar School profiles).
- **Pricing**: **15,000 PKR / month** per listing slot. Target of 10 active sponsors in Year 1.

## 3. Financial Scenarios (Month 12 Milestone)

Projections are calculated at an exchange rate of **1 USD = 278 PKR**:

### Scenario A: Conservative Case (Low User Adoption)
- **Monthly Active Users (MAU)**: 10,000
- **Pro Subscribers**: 1.0% -> 100 subscribers
- **Monthly Ad Impressions**: 9,900 free users * 60 impressions = 594,000 impressions
- **Ad Revenue**: (594.0k impressions / 1000) * $0.40 CPM * 278 = **66,053 PKR ($237.60 USD)**
- **Subscription Revenue**: 100 * 200 = **20,000 PKR ($71.94 USD)**
- **Academy Placements**: 3 sponsors * 15,000 = **45,000 PKR ($161.87 USD)**
- **Referral Leads**: 10,000 MAU * 0.15% * 1,000 PKR = **15,000 PKR ($53.96 USD)**
- **Total MRR**: **146,053 PKR ($525.37 USD)**
- **Total ARR**: **1,752,636 PKR ($6,304.44 USD)**

### Scenario B: Base Case (Target Performance)
- **Monthly Active Users (MAU)**: 40,000
- **Pro Subscribers**: 1.0% -> 400 subscribers
- **Monthly Ad Impressions**: 39,600 free users * 60 impressions = 2,376,000 impressions
- **Ad Revenue**: (2,376.0k impressions / 1000) * $0.40 CPM * 278 = **264,211 PKR ($950.40 USD)**
- **Subscription Revenue**: 400 * 200 = **80,000 PKR ($287.77 USD)**
- **Academy Placements**: 10 sponsors * 15,000 = **150,000 PKR ($539.57 USD)**
- **Referral Leads**: 40,000 MAU * 0.25% * 1,000 PKR = **100,000 PKR ($359.71 USD)**
- **Total MRR**: **594,211 PKR ($2,137.45 USD)**
- **Total ARR**: **7,130,532 PKR ($25,649.40 USD)**

### Scenario C: Optimistic Case (High Virality)
- **Monthly Active Users (MAU)**: 120,000
- **Pro Subscribers**: 1.2% -> 1,440 subscribers
- **Monthly Ad Impressions**: 118,560 free users * 60 impressions = 7,113,600 impressions
- **Ad Revenue**: (7,113.6k impressions / 1000) * $0.45 CPM (scale) * 278 = **889,911 PKR ($3,201.12 USD)**
- **Subscription Revenue**: 1,440 * 200 = **288,000 PKR ($1,035.97 USD)**
- **Academy Placements**: 25 sponsors * 15,000 = **375,000 PKR ($1,348.92 USD)**
- **Referral Leads**: 120,000 MAU * 0.35% * 1,000 PKR = **420,000 PKR ($1,510.79 USD)**
- **Total MRR**: **1,972,911 PKR ($7,096.80 USD)**
- **Total ARR**: **23,674,932 PKR ($85,161.60 USD)**
